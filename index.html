How it works

From a messy chat to a tidy library

  1. Bring them in

    Export a chat and share it to Stickfolio. Stickers and GIFs come in and copies are skipped. Photos, Files and the share sheet work too.

  2. Find any of them

    Type a word from the sticker, an emoji, or what it shows. Favourites and recently sent are one tap away.

  3. Send or make your own

    Tap to copy, or use the keyboard. Cut a sticker out of a photo, write a text sticker, or caption a meme.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do I get my stickers out of WhatsApp?

Open a chat in WhatsApp, tap its name, tap Export Chat, then Attach Media, and choose Stickfolio. The stickers and GIFs from that chat appear in your library. iPhone does not let one app read another's files, so this is done one chat at a time.

Does Stickfolio read my messages?

No. Only stickers and GIFs are taken from a chat export. Your messages are never read, and personal photos are left out unless you turn on looking for memes.

Why is my collection split into several packs?

WhatsApp packs hold 3 to 30 stickers, and still and animated stickers cannot share a pack. Stickfolio splits the collection for you.
