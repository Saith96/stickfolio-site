Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026.

In short: Stickfolio has no accounts, no servers and no ads, and we run no analytics. We do not collect your data ourselves. Your stickers, memes and GIFs stay on your device unless you send them somewhere yourself.

What stays on your device

Everything you add to Stickfolio, your collections, your favorites and your settings are stored only on your phone. We cannot see them. If you delete the app or use Settings → Erase Everything, they are removed.

Chat exports

When you share a WhatsApp chat export with Stickfolio, the app takes the stickers and GIFs out of it on your device. Your messages are not read. Photos and videos are left out, unless you turn on Look for Memes in Chat Exports; then pictures with words on them are kept as memes. The exported file is deleted from Stickfolio as soon as it has been read. Nothing is uploaded.

Reading stickers for search

To make search work, Stickfolio reads the words on each picture and recognises what it shows. This is done by your phone's own software, on the device. The results are stored only on your phone.

On Android, this software is Google's ML Kit, which comes with Google Play services. Your pictures never leave the phone, but ML Kit sends Google diagnostic information about how it is working: device and app information, identifiers, performance metrics and error codes. Google uses it to keep ML Kit running well. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

The keyboard

The Stickfolio keyboard shows your stickers inside other apps. It has no letter keys, does not read or store anything you type, and has no connection to the internet. iOS asks for Allow Full Access because a keyboard needs it to put a sticker on the clipboard and to read the library the Stickfolio app keeps on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Photos

When you add photos yourself, Stickfolio only sees the ones you pick in the system photo picker. Find Stickers in My Photos is different: if you start it, Stickfolio asks for access to your photo library and looks through your newest photos on your device to suggest the ones that would make good stickers. The photos are analysed by your phone's own software. They are not uploaded, and nothing is copied into Stickfolio until you choose it. You can decline, give access to selected photos only, or take access away at any time in Settings.

Sending

When you copy or share something, or add a pack to WhatsApp or Telegram, it goes from your phone straight to the app you chose. From there, that app's own privacy policy applies.

Backups

A backup is a file you create and keep wherever you choose. We never receive it.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple on iPhone and by Google Play on Android. We do not receive your payment details. Their own privacy policies apply to purchases.

Children

Stickfolio does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: saithdevelopments@gmail.com.
